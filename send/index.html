Nothing sends by itself. Each green button opens WhatsApp with the message prefilled for that guest — you review and press send there. Blue Open in Messages buttons mark guests whose number has no WhatsApp account (verified 2 Nov): they open Apple Messages (iMessage/SMS) instead — on an iPhone the text is prefilled; on a Mac it's copied automatically, so just paste (⌘V) and send. A row is ticked automatically when you open its chat; untick if you didn't send. Progress is shared: every tick is saved to the "Send Progress" tab of the ASHTH Launch RSVPs Google Sheet and syncs between everyone's devices. To add a guest, type their phone number (digits with country code) and name into a new row of that sheet — they appear here automatically.
First time? How to connect WhatsApp on this device
  1. On a phone: the green buttons open the WhatsApp app directly. If it's not installed, get it free from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
  2. On a Mac or Windows computer: download the free WhatsApp app from whatsapp.com/download (on a Mac you can also search "WhatsApp" in the App Store; on Windows, the Microsoft Store).
  3. No install needed: you can also use WhatsApp in the browser at web.whatsapp.com.
  4. Link it to your phone (first time only): open WhatsApp on the computer — it shows a QR code. On your phone open WhatsApp → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device, and scan the QR code on the computer screen.
Once linked, every green button below opens a chat with the invitation pre-written — you just review and press send. Guests shown with a blue Open in Messages button aren't on WhatsApp at all: those open Apple Messages (iMessage/SMS) instead, so no WhatsApp is needed for them.
Adding a recipient? New guests are added in the Google Sheet guest list, not here: type their phone number (digits with country code, e.g. 352691123456) in column A and their name in column B of a new row. They appear in this list automatically within a minute.
